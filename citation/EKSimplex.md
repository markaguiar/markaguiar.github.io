#### Abstract

We present a deterministic re-interpretation of the Eaton and Kortum (2002) Ricardian model of international trade. The representation can be interpreted as a Dornbusch–Fischer–Samuelson generalization, amenable to diagrammatic analysis, to an N-country world under specific technological restrictions that nest Eaton-Kortum.
